Product Summary
The European Union’s Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR) is fundamentally rewriting the rules of global trade. By mandating Digital Product Passports (DPP) for priority sectors like batteries, textiles, and steel, the EU requires manufacturers to provide unprecedented transparency into a product’s lifecycle, material composition, and environmental impact. However, for most enterprises, the reality of DPP compliance is a fragmented nightmare of siloed ERPs, unverified supplier claims, and manual data entry.
SovereignTrace is a next-generation, Agentic AI platform engineered to solve this exact challenge. We provide end-to-end software development partnerships, transforming the complex data requirements of the ESPR into a seamless, secure, and verifiable digital workflow. SovereignTrace does not just store data; it actively orchestrates it.
At its core, SovereignTrace leverages autonomous AI agents to continuously query, validate, and map supply chain data from raw material extraction to end-of-life recycling. These agents integrate directly with existing IoT platforms, geospatial tracking systems, and legacy databases. They automatically capture and verify critical DPP data points—such as carbon footprints, critical raw material origins, recycled content percentages, and substances of concern—ensuring ground-truth accuracy without manual intervention.
Recognizing that supply chain data is highly sensitive, SovereignTrace is built on a highly encrypted, open-source, decentralized architecture. Moving away from vulnerable centralized cloud silos, we utilize Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) frameworks and advanced cryptographic authentication. This ensures strict, role-based access control. When a consumer scans a product’s physical data carrier, they see verified sustainability metrics. When a repairer scans it, they access detailed disassembly guides. Public authorities can instantly verify compliance, all while the manufacturer’s proprietary supplier networks and trade secrets remain completely encrypted and confidential.
As a dedicated technology partner, SovereignTrace supports your business from initial compliance hypothesis to full-scale delivery. We don't just hand over a software license; we build the autonomous workflows that future-proof your operations against evolving EU delegated acts. 
Beyond mere compliance, SovereignTrace shifts the narrative of the circular economy from a regulatory burden to a strategic advantage. By automating data orchestration, we empower brands to definitively prove their sustainability claims, avoid costly border rejections, and unlock new circular business models. SovereignTrace is where decentralized trust meets autonomous automation, providing the verified, ground-truth data required to thrive in the modern, transparent market.
